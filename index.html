About

Thinking across disciplines.

I’m interested in the intersection between technical systems and human systems — how people behave, how ideas spread, how technologies reshape incentives, and how complexity changes decision-making.

My projects range from robotics and software engineering to long-form essays about cognition, ethics, productivity, and modern technological culture.

This site exists partly as a portfolio, but mostly as a structured thinking environment. A place to publish unfinished ideas publicly and improve them over time.
